    How GLP-1 Microdosing Works in Riverside

    Microdosing is a dosing strategy, not a separate drug. It means taking semaglutide or tirzepatide at doses lower than the standard labeled schedule, or increasing the dose more slowly, while the medicine still works on the same gut hormone receptors. Some people notice quieter food noise. Because brand pens come in fixed doses, low-dose plans often use compounded medication, which is not FDA-approved.

    Who GLP-1 Microdosing Is For

    Three groups ask about it most. The typical candidates had trouble with nausea on standard doses, prefer a gentler start or are trying to hold onto weight they already lost. It does not bypass medical criteria. Claims that microdosing treats inflammation or aging are not backed by strong evidence.

    Low-Dose Schedule in Riverside

    There is no single standard schedule. A common approach begins at a fraction of the labeled starting dose, like part of 0.25 mg semaglutide or 2.5 mg tirzepatide, and moves up in smaller increments. Most plans stay weekly. Never adjust the dose on your own.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Early weeks vary a lot between people. Some notice less food noise and earlier fullness within the first two weeks, while others notice almost nothing at a very low dose and need a small increase to feel an effect. Nausea is usually milder. Expect modest, gradual change.

    Side Effects in Riverside

    Lower doses reduce side effects but do not erase them. Nausea, constipation, diarrhea, reflux and reduced appetite are still the most common, especially in the days after any dose increase, even a small one. The boxed warning remains. GLP-1 risks like pancreatitis and gallstones, and the rodent thyroid tumor warning, do not go away at low doses.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening is the same as for full doses. The review covers BMI, weight history, diagnoses, medications and earlier GLP-1 use, with questions about the dose you reached and what made you stop. MEN 2 is a hard stop. Past pancreatitis or an eating disorder needs a longer conversation.

    Microdosing Compared With Standard GLP-1 Treatment in Riverside

    Standard dosing has the trial data. The STEP and SURMOUNT trials tested full doses and reported average losses near 15 to 20 percent, and nothing comparable exists for low-dose plans. Effects are likely more modest. Maintenance is a common use case.
