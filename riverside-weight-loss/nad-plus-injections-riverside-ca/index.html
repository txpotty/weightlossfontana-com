    How NAD+ Injection Therapy Works in Riverside, CA

    How NAD+ Works in Riverside

    NAD+ is nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide. In every cell it carries electrons through the reactions that turn food into ATP, and it is consumed by sirtuins and PARPs, enzymes tied to DNA repair and stress responses. Levels decline with age. A subcutaneous injection places NAD+ under the skin, where it is absorbed into the bloodstream without passing through the digestive tract, though how much reaches each tissue is still being studied.

    Who NAD+ Injections Are Usually For

    Typical patients want steadier energy, clearer thinking or better recovery. People on semaglutide or tirzepatide sometimes add it. It is not a weight loss medication and does not treat any specific disease, and people who are pregnant, breastfeeding, have active cancer or significant liver or kidney disease are generally not candidates.

    NAD+ Dosing and Schedule in Riverside

    Small shots, slow push. Many compounded protocols begin around 50 milligrams two or three times a week and rise gradually, often toward 100 milligrams or more, as tolerance builds. Use the belly or thigh with a fine needle. Pushing the plunger over about a minute cuts down on flushing.

    What to Expect in the First Month

    Week one is about technique. Many people feel warmth, flushing, mild nausea or a tight feeling right after injecting, and it usually fades within minutes. By the end of the first month, responders describe more even energy, and plenty of people feel no difference. Your provider adjusts the ramp at check-ins.

    NAD+ Injection Side Effects in Riverside

    Flushing leads the list. Injection site redness, stinging and soreness, along with nausea, lightheadedness and headache, are also common. Chest tightness, muscle cramps or a racing heart can happen if the dose is too high or pushed too fast, and these usually improve with a lower dose or slower injection. Hives or trouble breathing mean call 911.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The review starts with history and your supplement list. Expect questions on cancer, pregnancy, organ health, heart rhythm and whether you use NMN, NR or high-dose niacin. Labs are not always required. Upload recent labs if you have them.

    NAD+ Injections Compared to Other Options in Riverside

    IV infusions are the usual comparison. IV NAD+ puts more in at once, though sessions often last hours and the discomfort during the drip is a common complaint. The nasal spray is needle-free. NMN and NR capsules work differently, as building blocks.
